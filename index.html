On the 17th of August, 2004 at Banay, Sta. Cruz Ilocos Sur a cute baby girl was born and her name is Maegan Cabato De Vega. I was 20 years old turning 21 this year and a 3rd year college student of BS in Computer Science at ISPSC Main Campus. My parents were Mr. Reynaldo A. De Vega and Mrs. Marilou C. De Vega. I have two siblings and we are all girls so that they called us the tres maria’s of the De Vega family or the three roses. I was the middle of three siblings and our family are all live in a family house of my mother. The name of my siblings is Ritz Ann Yvone De Vega she is the eldest and the youngest is Zaira Mae De Vega. And also, we have our supportive grandmother Emelia Cabato.

My first attended school when I was 4 years old, I started at Banay Day Care Center in our Barangay. When I was in elementary day, I studied at San Jose Elementary School in San Jose, Sta. Cruz, Ilocos Sur when I was in Kindergarten, Grade 1, 2, 3, 5, and 6. In grade 4 I transferred in Banay Primary School which is Banay Elementary School now. In High School in our batch, they started changing the curriculum that they have a Junior High School grade 7 to 10 and a Senior High School grade 11 and 12. I studied at San Jose High School and graduated in same school but they change it in San Jose High School Inc. Now, I am a 3rd year college student took a degree in Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at ISPSC Main Campus.

In the year 2020 there have an unexpected situation that change our lifestyle. I was in grade 10 when the pandemic came and the graduation date is near but because of the virus or the covid-19 they didn’t continue to take our graduation. In our batch we are the completers that day so that we can’t experience to become completers but for our graduation we experience it. We take modular learning when we are at senior high to study at home to avoid the said virus.

At present I am one of the Sk officer member in our brgy. but unexpected situation our sk chairperson resigned and I am her first kagawad so that I am the one who took her position. Now I am the sk chairperson in our brgy. and a college student. And also, a member of the IYM in our church the St. Joseph the Worker Parish.
